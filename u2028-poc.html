Bug: U+2028 is Unicode category Zl and U+2029 is Zp. Chrome's filename sanitizer replaces Cc/Cf but not Zl/Zp, so the character survives. Android's single-line Downloads title treats it as a mandatory line break: the first line (invoice.txt) is shown, the real extension on the second line is hidden.

Not a bug if: the title shows invoice.txt .pdf / invoice.txt .apk (the separator was replaced with a space), or both lines are visible. In that case the candidate is not exploitable on your build.
